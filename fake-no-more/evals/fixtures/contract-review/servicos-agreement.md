# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between NORTHWIND LOGISTICS LTD ("Client") and PALLAS DATA SYSTEMS SA ("Supplier").
Executed 12 March 2025. Commencement date: 1 April 2025.

## 1. Definitions
1.1 "Services" means the services described in Schedule A.
1.2 "Personal Data" has the meaning given in the applicable data protection law.

## 2. Term
2.1 This Agreement runs for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months from the
Commencement Date.
2.2 It renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless
terminated under Clause 9.

## 3. Charges
3.1 Charges are set out in Schedule B and invoiced monthly in arrears.
3.2 Charges may be increased once per contract year, capped at CPI + 2%.

## 4. Service Levels
4.1 Supplier shall maintain 99.5% monthly availability.
4.2 Service credits for breach of 4.1 are set out in Schedule C and are the
Client's sole remedy for availability failures.

## 7. Confidentiality
7.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for the
term and for three (3) years thereafter.

## 8. Liability
8.1 Supplier's aggregate liability is capped at the Charges paid in the twelve
(12) months preceding the claim.
8.2 Nothing limits liability for death, personal injury, or fraud.

## 9. Termination
9.1 Either party may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.
9.2 Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not remedied
within fifteen (15) days of notice.

## 10. Data Protection
10.1 The parties shall comply with applicable data protection law.
10.2 Details of processing are set out in Annex B (Data Processing Terms).
