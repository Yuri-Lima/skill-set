# Internal memo — Northwind / Pallas MSA
To: Procurement
From: Legal ops
Date: 8 August 2026

Summary of the Pallas MSA ahead of the renewal decision.

1. **Term.** Initial term of 24 months, auto-renewing annually.

2. **Exit.** We can walk away for convenience on **90 days' notice** (Clause 9.1).
   Practically this means we must decide by early January.

3. **Liability.** Capped at 12 months of charges (Clause 8.1). Uncapped for
   death, injury and fraud.

4. **Data protection.** The agreement has **no data processing clause** — this is
   a gap we should close at renewal, especially given the ICO's 2024 guidance on
   processor contracts requiring documented processing terms.

5. **Price protection.** Increases capped at CPI + 2%, once per year (Clause 3.2).

6. **Audit rights.** We have a right to audit the Supplier annually under
   Clause 14.2.

7. **Availability.** 99.5% monthly. Service credits are our sole remedy.

Recommendation: renew, but negotiate a DPA and extend the termination notice.
